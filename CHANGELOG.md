## 1.0.0

The first flock.

- **Zed**: Dodo Dusk, Night, Obsidian, Dawn and Mist, plus five matching icon
  themes (168 SVGs each, 400+ file associations, 140+ named folders).
- **Terminals**: Terminal.app, iTerm2 (with light/dark auto-switching
  profiles), Ghostty, Kitty, Alacritty, WezTerm, Warp.
- **Xcode**: five `.xccolortheme`s that soften SDK symbols relative to your
  own.
- `install.sh`, `update.sh` (with launchd / systemd / cron auto-updates) and
  `uninstall.sh`, with a pixel-art dodo banner.
